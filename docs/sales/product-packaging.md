# TechGemini — Product Packaging & Pricing Presentation

How to present TechGemini's value and plans to prospects. Recommendations are always framed as **directional guidance** — never guaranteed outcomes.

## Value proposition

TechGemini turns a student's academics, subjects, interests and (optional) assessments into a structured, research-backed map of:
- **Recommended career paths** — ranked, with plain-language reasons.
- **Study pathways** — degrees, specializations and subjects that prepare for those careers.
- **Relevant universities** — whose programs align with the chosen direction.
- **Counselor guidance** — a workflow to discuss and refine options.

## Who it's for

- **Schools & institutes** that want structured career guidance for their students.
- **Counselors** who need a reliable starting point for each student.
- **Students & families** who want clear, actionable next steps.

## Plans

Plan details (tiers, limits, features) are **configurable** per customer and stored in TechGemini's subscription model, so the presented plan is always tailored to the contract — not a fixed claim.

- **Trial — 14 days.** A working, paid-equivalent trial to experience the full journey before committing. Trial-expired organizations are clearly told to upgrade to continue adding capacity.
- **Professional (and other paid tiers).** Paid plans set your limits on counselors and students and unlock reports, university recommendations and full counselor features.

Representatives should present the **specific plan** for the customer with its **included features**, **student/counselor limits**, and **per-seat usage** — reading limits straight from the org's Billing/Plan view rather than guessing.

## Configurable presentation

- Because plans are per-customer and configurable, always confirm the customer's **plan type**, **limits** and **included features** before the call.
- Show the **Billing / Plan** screen in the demo so the prospect sees exactly what their contract includes.

## Guardrails

- Never present non-existent features.
- Don't invent pricing; present the configured plan and let the commercial team quote.
- Recommend ≥ not commit: "directional guidance" / "strong alignment" / "recommended paths", never "guaranteed", "prediction", "admission/salary outcome", or "100% accuracy".
- Demo data is synthetic; onboarding and guides are in `docs/customer/`.
