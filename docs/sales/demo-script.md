# TechGemini — Demo Script

A ~15-minute scripted demonstration of TechGemini using the built-in synthetic "TechGemini Demo School". Every person in it is **fake** (`@demo.techgemini.local`). Always sign in with the demo password `DemoPass2026!`.

## Setup (before the call)
- Open the public landing page.
- Have the demo org ready (re-seed with `node scripts/seed-phase19-demo-org.js` if needed).

## 1. Hook — the problem (0:00–2:00)
> "Students and families often struggle to turn grades and interests into clear next steps. TechGemini gives counselors a structured, research-backed starting point for every student — so guidance starts from useful matches, not a blank page."

## 2. The student experience (2:00–6:00)
Show a student's profile:
- Academics, subjects, interests and study goals.
- (Optional) Assessment answers.

Then show the outputs:
- **Recommended careers** ranked by "how well they fit what the student shared," each with a short reason.
- **Study pathways** — degrees, specializations, subjects.
- **Universities** whose programs align.

> Say: "These are **recommended career paths to explore**, with strong alignment to what the student shared — directional guidance they discuss with a counselor, not a guaranteed outcome."

## 3. The counselor experience (6:00–10:00)
Sign in as a counselor (e.g., `counselor1@demo.techgemini.local`):
- Open a student's **360 view**.
- Review the ranked careers and reasons.
- Show **follow-up notes** and how a **session** is booked.

> "The counselor sees the reasoning behind each recommendation, so they can talk trade-offs with the student and family — not just recite a list."

## 4. The organization admin experience (10:00–13:00)
Sign in as the org admin (`admin@demo.techgemini.local`):
- **Overview** — counselors, students, assessments, plan.
- **Students** and **Counselors** management.
- **Billing / plan** — limits and included features.

> "Admins see clear usage and their plan entitlements, and counselor/student seats are managed in one place."

## 5. Closing & next step (13:00–15:00)
- Summarize: structured guidance for students, a productive workflow for counselors, control for admins.
- Offer **Start Trial (14 days)** or **Request a Demo / Contact Sales** from the public site.
- Share the onboarding checklist and customer guides.

## Talking-point guardrails
- ✅ "recommended career paths", "strong alignment", "explore these options", "discuss with your counselor".
- ❌ never "guaranteed career/prediction/admission/salary", "100% accuracy", "perfect match".
- ✅ demo data is clearly synthetic; never demo with real student info.
