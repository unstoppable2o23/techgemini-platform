# TechGemini — Objection Handling

*Customer-facing sales guidance. Always keep recommendations framed as **directional guidance**, not a guaranteed outcome.*

## 1. "Isn't this just another career test?"

**Acknowledge, then differentiate.** Career tests give a one-time output and stop. TechGemini is a *connected workflow*: assessment → recommended careers → study pathways → universities → a counselor who reviews and follows up. The value is the outcome for the student, not a single score.

## 2. "How do I know the recommendations are right?"

Be honest and confident: recommendations are based on the student's own academics, subjects, interests and (optional) assessment answers, and each one includes a plain-language reason explaining *why* it fits. They are **recommended paths to explore** that the student brings to a counselor — not a guaranteed outcome. We do not overclaim, and counselors make the final judgment with the student.

## 3. "We already have career counselors doing this by hand."

Agree that counselors are essential — TechGemini is designed for them. It turns a blank page into a structured starting point with ranked options and reasons, frees time for meaningful discussion, and keeps notes/actions/follow-ups in one place. Counselors stay in control.

## 4. "Will it help our students get into university / get jobs?"

Keep it accurate: TechGemini maps careers to **study pathways and universities whose programs align** with the student's direction. It does **not** guarantee admission, salary, or a job. The honest answer builds trust — the value is clarity and a decided next step, not a promise.

## 5. "Selling a trial just leads to a hard outbound pitch."

We start with a live demo and a 14-day trial that actually works end-to-end, so the prospect sees value first. Onboarding (add admin → counselors → students → students assess → counselors review) is minutes, not weeks. Upgrade is a natural next step once they see results, not a surprise.

## 6. "Will my team be able to use it quickly?"

Yes — plain-language UI, concise customer guides, and a short onboarding checklist. The salesperson completes a demo in ~10 minutes; an admin can go from sign-in to counselors + students in one sitting.

## 7. "What about my plan limits on students/counselors?"

Plans are configured per customer and shown in the Admin dashboard's Billing / Plan screen. Limits (counselors, students) and included features are per contract, represented directly in the product — not invented on the spot.

## Guardrails

- ✅ "recommended career paths", "strong alignment", "explore these options", "discuss with your counselor".
- ❌ "guaranteed career/admission/salary/job", "100% accuracy", "perfect prediction".
- Demo data is synthetic; never demonstrate with real student information.
