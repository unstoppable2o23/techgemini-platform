# TechGemini — Product One-Pager

*Concise sales-facing summary. Recommendations are always **directional guidance** — never a guaranteed outcome, admission, or salary.*

## Problem

Students and families struggle to turn grades, interests and strengths into clear next steps. Career tests are disconnected from university searches; counselors start from blank pages; institutions have no single workflow to guide students from assessment to a decided path.

## TechGemini solution

One connected guidance platform that takes a student from assessment to decisions:

**Assessment → Recommended Careers → Study Pathways → Universities → Counselor Guidance**

Everything lives in one calm, guided workflow, so no step is disconnected from the next.

## Workflow

1. **Assess** — student shares academics, subjects, interests and (optional) assessment answers.
2. **Discover careers** — careers ranked by how well they fit the shared information, with plain-language reasons.
3. **Explore programs** — degrees, specializations and subjects that prepare for those careers.
4. **Explore universities** — institutions whose programs align with the direction.
5. **Counselor guidance** — a counselor reviews each student's matches, adds notes, and books follow-up sessions.

## Customer types

- **Schools & higher-ed institutions** — structured career guidance for their students.
- **Career counselors / consultancies** — a reliable starting point for each student.
- **Students & families** — clear, actionable next steps.

## Key benefits

- **For students:** recommendations they understand, with reasons, plus concrete study and university options.
- **For counselors:** a research-backed 360° starting point and a workflow for notes, actions and follow-up.
- **For admins:** org dashboard, counselor/student seats, and plan/entitlement visibility in one place.
- **For institutions:** tenant isolation and per-organization control; everyone sees only their own students.

## Implementation / onboarding

From **Request Demo or Start Trial** a new organization is created and the admin logs in, adds counselors and students, students complete assessments, and counselors review results. Full onboarding checklist: `docs/customer/onboarding-checklist.md`.
