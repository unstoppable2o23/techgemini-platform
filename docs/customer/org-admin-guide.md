# TechGemini — Organization Admin Guide

For the administrator of a school / institute that uses TechGemini to guide students' career and study decisions. This guide is customer-facing; it uses plain language (no internal terms).

## Your role as Organization Admin

As the organization admin you manage your institution's TechGemini space: who can sign in, the counselors who support students, your students, and your plan/billing. You are the single owner of the organization account and its settings.

## Sign in

1. Go to your organization's sign-in page and sign in with your org-admin email.
2. You are directed to your **Admin dashboard**, the home for your organization.

## Your admin dashboard

- **Overview** — a snapshot of your organization: number of counselors, number of students, assessments completed, career results, study roadmaps, follow-ups required, and your current plan. It also shows **pilot metrics** (invitations pending/accepted, students with shortlisted universities) so you can measure how far along your launch is.
- **Students** — view and manage the students in your organization. You can **invite** students to set up their own sign-in and **import** them in bulk from a CSV.
- **Counselors** — add, view, and manage the career counselors who work with your students.
- **Billing / plan** — see your current plan, usage versus your plan's limits, and what features your plan includes.
- **Setup** — a guided, 5-step checklist (organization → counselors → students → configuration → launch) that takes you from a new account to a live, fully-invited cohort.

## Set up your organization (setup wizard)

The first time you sign in you will see a **Setup** banner pointing you to the guided wizard. It walks through five steps:

1. **Organization** — confirm your organization name and branding (colors and logo).
2. **Counselors** — add the career counselors who will guide your students.
3. **Students** — add your students individually or import them from a CSV.
4. **Configure** — choose started settings such as inviting students.
5. **Launch** — review the checklist and mark yourself ready to invite your students.

The wizard tracks your progress. You can leave and return anytime; completed steps are remembered.

## Add students with the CSV import

Instead of typing students one by one, you can upload a CSV file:

- Prepare a header row with columns such as **firstName**, **lastName**, **email**, **phone**, **gradeLevel**, and (optionally) **counselor** (the counselor's email).
- From the **Students** tab choose **Import CSV** and upload your file.
- TechGemini validates every row and reports clear errors for any row that is missing a name, has an invalid email, duplicates an existing student, or references an unknown counselor.
- Valid rows are created as student accounts. A sample CSV file is provided so you can copy its format.

## Invite students to sign in

Each student account needs its own sign-in.

- From the **Students** tab, choose **Invite** on a student.
- The student receives a secure, time-limited **invitation link** (valid 7 days). No passwords are ever sent by email.
- The student opens the link and sets their own password. Once they do, the invitation is shown as accepted and their account is active.
- Inviting a student again creates a fresh link and invalidates the old one.

## Contact support

Use **Help & Support** (from the menu in the top-right, or the Support link on the dashboard) to ask a question, report a problem, or request contact. Your request is saved under your organization so we can respond with the right context.

## Add a counselor

1. From the Admin dashboard, open **Counselors**.
2. Choose **Add counselor** and enter the counselor's details (name and email).
3. The counselor receives access under your organization. They can then sign in and start reviewing students.
4. Adding a counselor counts against your plan's counselor limit. Your dashboard shows how many counselor seats you have used and remain.

## View and manage students

- Your **Students** view lists the students in your organization and their status.
- Counselors you have added can review each student's recommended career paths, study pathways and university options, add follow-up notes, and book guidance sessions.
- The number of students you can have active is set by your plan.

## Billing and plan

- Your **Billing / plan** view shows your current plan, its limits (number of counselors, number of students) and the features it includes (reports, university recommendations, counselor features).
- The demo organization runs on the paid **Professional** plan, so it never hits trial limits during a demonstration.

## Keeping student data safe

- Only people you authorize (org admin and your counselors) can see your students' information.
- We do not collect more personal data than needed, and recommendations are guidance — not a guaranteed outcome.

## Help

If something is unclear, contact our support team. We do not sell or misuse student data.
