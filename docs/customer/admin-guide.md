# TechGemini — Admin Guide

*This is an alias of [`org-admin-guide.md`](./org-admin-guide.md). It is the same content kept under the conventional `admin-guide.md` name.*

For the administrator of a school / institute using TechGemini to guide students' career and study decisions. Plain customer-facing language (no internal terms).

## Your role as Organization Admin

You manage your institution's TechGemini space: who can sign in, the counselors who support students, your students, and your plan/billing. You are the single owner of the organization account and its settings.

## Sign in

1. Go to your organization's sign-in page and sign in with your org-admin email.
2. You are directed to your **Admin dashboard**.

## Your admin dashboard

- **Overview** — snapshot of your organization: counselors, students, assessments completed, counselor notes/follow-ups, and your current plan.
- **Students** — view and manage the students in your organization.
- **Counselors** — add, view, and manage the career counselors who work with your students.
- **Billing / plan** — your current plan, usage versus limits, and which features are included.

## Add a counselor

1. Open **Counselors** from the Admin dashboard.
2. Choose **Add counselor** and enter name + email.
3. The counselor can then sign in and start reviewing students.
4. Adding a counselor counts against your plan's counselor limit (shown in the dashboard).

## View and manage students

- Your **Students** view lists the students in your organization and their status.
- Counselors you add can review each student's recommended career paths, study pathways, and university options, add follow-up notes, and book guidance sessions.
- Your plan sets the maximum number of active students.

## Billing and plan

- Your **Billing / plan** view shows your plan, its limits (counselors, students) and the features it includes (reports, university recommendations, counselor features).
- The demo organization runs on the paid **Professional** plan, so it never hits trial limits during a demonstration.

## Keeping student data safe

- Only people you authorize (org admin and your counselors) can see your students' information.
- We collect only what's needed for useful guidance — recommendations are guidance, not a guaranteed outcome.

## Help

Contact our support team if anything is unclear. We do not sell or misuse student data.
