# TechGemini — Counselor Guide

For career and study counselors guiding students inside TechGemini. Plain-language, customer-facing.

## Your role as a Counselor

You help students make sense of their career options. TechGemini gives you a structured, research-backed starting point for each student — a snapshot of careers that most strongly align with what they've shared — so your conversations start from useful matches instead of a blank page.

## Your students

- Your list shows the students assigned to you.
- Each student has a profile describing their academics, subjects, interests and (optional) assessment answers.

## The student 360 view

When you open a student, you see a complete picture:

- **Recommended career paths** — careers ranked by how well they fit the information the student shared, with short reasons why each fits.
- **Study pathways** — degrees, specializations and subjects that prepare a student for those careers.
- **University options** — institutions whose programs align with the student's direction.
- **Notes & follow-ups** — your notes and next steps for that student.

Recommendations are **directional guidance**. They are a starting point for discussion, not a promise of outcomes. Use them to explore options with the student and their family.

## What you can do for a student

1. **Review their recommended careers** and discuss which feel like a good fit for their goals and circumstances.
2. **Recommend study pathways** and universities that align with their interests.
3. **Add follow-up notes** so nothing is lost between sessions.
4. **Book a session or send a message** when a student or family wants a deeper conversation.

## Privacy

You can only see students assigned to your organization. TechGemini does not share students' data beyond their organization, and we collect only what's needed to give useful guidance.

## Keep it human

Your guidance matters. Treat the ranked careers as suggestions to explore — not as a final verdict — and help each student arrive at a direction that fits their priorities, not just the numbers.
