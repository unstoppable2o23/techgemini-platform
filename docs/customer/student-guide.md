# TechGemini — Student Guide

For students using TechGemini to explore careers and study paths. Plain-language, customer-facing.

## What TechGemini does

TechGemini helps you discover career directions and study paths that fit **your** interests, subjects, strengths and goals — then shows you degrees, universities and counselors to help you move forward.

It's a **guided starting point**, not a fortune-teller. Recommendations are based on the information you share, and they are directional — they help you explore options, but they don't guarantee any particular outcome, admission or salary.

## Get started

1. **Create a profile** — share a few details about your academics, subjects, interests and study goals.
2. **(Optional) Take assessments** — answer the interest and personality questions. More good answers help your matches be more useful.
3. **Review your recommended careers** — careers ranked by how well they fit what you shared, with simple reasons why each fits.
4. **Explore study pathways** — see the degrees, specializations and subjects that prepare you for those careers.
5. **Explore universities** — look at institutions whose programs align with your direction.
6. **Talk to a counselor** — shortlist, compare, and book a session to discuss your options with someone who can guide you in person.

## How to get the most from it

- Be honest in your answers — matches are only as good as what you share.
- Treat the ranked careers as **options to explore**, not a final verdict.
- Use the "why this fits" explanations to understand *why* a career is suggested, not just *that* it is.
- Bring your matches to your counselor to talk through trade-offs and what matters most to you.

## Your school and privacy

- Your school's counselors can see your profile and recommended options to help guide you.
- We collect only what's needed to give useful guidance and do not sell your data.

## Remember

Your path is yours to choose. TechGemini is here to surface possibilities and structure the decision — not to decide for you.
